Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Karasu handles information when you visit or interact with this website.

Last updated: September 10, 2026.

Information you provide

Karasu does not require visitors to create an account or provide personal information in order to read the site.

If you contact Karasu by email, the information you provide may be retained for the purpose of responding to your message and maintaining relevant correspondence.

Analytics

Karasu uses Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the website and to improve its guides and content.

Google Analytics is not enabled until you choose to accept analytics through the analytics preference notice displayed on this website.

When analytics is enabled, information may include page visits, session activity, traffic source, approximate geographic information, browser and device information, and interactions with the website.

Google Analytics may use first-party cookies, including cookies used to distinguish users and maintain session information. Google processes analytics data according to its own terms and privacy policies.

Analytics preference

Your analytics preference is stored locally in your browser so that Karasu can remember whether you accepted or declined analytics.

Affiliate links

Some pages on Karasu may contain affiliate links. If you click one of these links and make a qualifying purchase or sign up, Karasu may earn a commission at no additional cost to you.

When analytics has been accepted, Karasu may record interactions with affiliate links in Google Analytics in order to understand which content and recommendations are useful to readers.

After you follow an affiliate link, the destination website or affiliate provider may process information according to its own privacy policy and terms.

External websites

Karasu may link to websites and services operated by third parties. Those websites operate under their own privacy policies and data practices.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Karasu changes the services, analytics tools, or data practices used on the website.
