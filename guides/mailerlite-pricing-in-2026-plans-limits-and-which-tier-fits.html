What is included in MailerLite Free?

The Free plan costs $0 and supports up to 250 active subscribers and 2,500 monthly emails. MailerLite also lists two user seats, up to three active automations, three forms, one landing page, one website of up to five pages, and one digital product. These limits make Free a reasonable starting point for a small list, as long as both the audience and sending volume remain within the caps.

If an account exceeds the subscriber or sending limit, MailerLite says sending and some related functions can be paused until the account is brought under the limit or upgraded. Free accounts are not automatically charged for crossing a limit; an upgrade is a user choice. Review the Free plan update FAQ for how account restrictions work.

MailerLite Comfort vs Power: how the plans differ

Comfort and Power share MailerLite’s core email marketing platform, but they differ in capacity and support. Comfort has defined sending and active-item limits. Power is listed with unlimited monthly sending (subject to fair use), unlimited feature use, and unlimited user seats. MailerLite’s FAQ describes email support for Comfort and live chat support for Power; live chat is also available on Comfort plans for 50,000 or more subscribers.

  • Choose Comfort for consideration when your team needs regular campaigns and automations, has no more than three user seats, and can stay within the plan’s sending and active-item limits.
  • Compare Power when forecast email volume or active features exceed Comfort’s caps, or when your team needs unlimited user seats. Whether the extra capacity is worth the price depends on your selected subscriber tier and actual usage.

Comfort includes up to 50 active automations, 10 forms, 10 websites and landing pages combined, and five digital products. Power’s unlimited feature use removes those stated Comfort caps. Limits apply to active or published items, not drafts. Check the current plan comparison before relying on a specific feature or support entitlement.

How monthly and annual MailerLite pricing works

Comfort starts at $12 per month and Power at $25 per month on the monthly option. MailerLite advertises a 10% discount for annual billing. The actual amount depends on the selected subscriber tier; an annual plan is a 12-month commitment billed upfront, and the displayed monthly equivalent should not be mistaken for a monthly payment.

MailerLite accepts USD and EUR, with currency determined from billing details. Applicable taxes may be added at checkout. Annual plans renew automatically unless canceled. Subscriber or sending growth can trigger an automatic move to a higher paid tier, with a prorated charge under the plan’s billing rules. Before subscribing, compare the same subscriber tier under monthly and annual billing and review the renewal amount, currency, taxes, and any eligible discount.

Why does MailerLite’s price change with subscriber count?

Comfort and Power are sold in subscriber tiers from 500 to 200,000. Larger lists cost more, so a starting price applies only to the entry tier and billing selection shown by MailerLite. Comfort’s monthly sending allowance also scales with the selected subscriber tier: it is 10 times that tier’s upper subscriber limit. For example, MailerLite’s FAQ gives 50,000 monthly emails for a 5,000-subscriber tier. Power’s monthly sending is unlimited subject to fair use.

MailerLite counts subscribers according to its account and billing rules. Check the subscriber total shown in your account and select the corresponding tier in the official price calculator rather than estimating from a list export.

What changed from the old MailerLite plans?

In 2026, MailerLite renamed Growing Business to Comfort and Advanced to Power, and updated paid-plan pricing, sending allowances, and feature limits. The Free plan also changed: its active-subscriber cap fell from 500 to 250, its monthly email allowance fell from 12,000 to 2,500, and the account seat limit increased from one to two.

Existing paid accounts do not all move to the new structure on the same day. MailerLite says the transition takes place at the next renewal or when an account is manually upgraded or downgraded. Older articles may therefore describe a legacy account’s current terms. Check the account’s Plan and billing page for its transition date and current tier.

Which MailerLite plan should you choose?

  • Free: choose it for a list of up to 250 active subscribers and up to 2,500 emails per 30-day cycle when the feature limits are enough.
  • Comfort: consider it for a growing newsletter or small team that needs more than Free but can forecast its volume within the tier’s sending allowance and feature caps.
  • Power: compare it when frequent campaigns, multiple collaborators, or active workflows make Comfort’s limits restrictive. Check fair-use terms and the price at your audience size.
  • Enterprise: contact MailerLite for a quote if your list exceeds 200,000 subscribers or you need custom sending volume and dedicated support.

Frequently asked questions about MailerLite pricing

What are the MailerLite Free plan limits?

What happened to Growing Business and Advanced?

Growing Business became Comfort, and Advanced became Power. Existing customers move at their next renewal or when they manually upgrade or downgrade. The account’s Plan and billing page shows which structure currently applies.
