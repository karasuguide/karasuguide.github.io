Small-business automation checklist

Use this checklist to find processes that may be worth automating. Start with one category and one workflow rather than connecting every tool at once.

Marketing

  • Capture form submissions into one system.
  • Tag leads by source or campaign.
  • Schedule repeatable reporting.
  • Notify the team when a high-intent lead arrives.

Sales

  • Create follow-up tasks after meetings.
  • Keep contact fields synchronized.
  • Generate reminders for stalled opportunities.

Customer support

  • Route requests by topic.
  • Attach account context to new tickets.
  • Escalate messages that match high-priority conditions.

Operations

  • Generate recurring status reports.
  • Archive files using predictable naming rules.
  • Alert on failed jobs rather than checking dashboards manually.

Before turning anything on

  • Define who owns the workflow.
  • Test with sample data.
  • Keep a manual fallback.
  • Log failures.
  • Review the automation after the first week.
