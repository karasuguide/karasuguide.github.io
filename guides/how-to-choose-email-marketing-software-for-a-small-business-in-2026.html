How to Choose Email Marketing Software for a Small Business in 2026

Compare AWeber, MailerLite, and Brevo by list size, sending needs, automation, and how much operational complexity your small business actually needs.

Quick verdict

Editorial view: AWeber suits businesses seeking conventional email marketing, autoresponders, forms, landing pages, integrations, and subscriber-based plans. Free and Lite have caps; Plus costs more for broader access.

Editorial view: MailerLite suits a cost-conscious business seeking campaigns, a website or landing page, and modest automation. Its updated Free plan caps at 250 active subscribers and 2,500 monthly emails.

Editorial view: Brevo suits businesses prioritizing send volume, transactional messages, or broader customer communication. Its volume-based plans are not directly comparable with subscriber-based allowances.

Which platform fits your small business?

A. Solo consultant or service business

If you need a signup form, a welcome sequence, and a regular newsletter, compare the simplest plan covering those tasks. AWeber is worth shortlisting if you prefer its subscriber-tier structure and want email, form, and landing-page tools together. MailerLite may suit a smaller list if Free or Comfort covers your forms, pages, and automations. Confirm that your plan allows enough active sequences.

B. Local or appointment-based business

Map marketing and operational messages. A newsletter platform can support promotions and follow-up, but appointment confirmations may require transactional sending and a booking-system integration. Brevo documents transactional email and SMS as well as marketing channels; verify setup and volume for your workflow. For AWeber or MailerLite, confirm the required booking integration before choosing.

C. Ecommerce or product seller

Prioritize the path from purchase or signup to a relevant customer message. Check whether your store connects directly or through an integration, which events can trigger automation, how consent and unsubscribes synchronize, and whether reports answer your questions. AWeber and MailerLite publish integration directories; Brevo may fit where campaign email sits alongside transactional or other channel needs. Compare the exact workflow rather than assuming feature parity.

D. Growing small team with automation needs

Estimate active sequences, users, forms, and pages. AWeber Plus lists broader access and a 12-times monthly send allowance based on subscriber tier. MailerLite Comfort has item caps, while Power lists unlimited feature use and monthly sends subject to fair use. Brevo Standard lists automation for unlimited contacts, A/B testing, and advanced reporting. Compare the triggers, seats, reports, and send volume your team needs.

AWeber for small businesses

AWeber is a reasonable shortlist choice for a business that wants campaigns, forms, landing pages, and autoresponders in one product family and prefers subscriber-tier pricing. Official materials list Free, Lite, Plus, and Unlimited. Free covers up to 500 subscribers and 3,000 monthly sends, with one list profile and one workflow automation or autoresponder campaign. That can suit a basic workflow, but the single-workflow boundary matters if you need concurrent sequences.

At 500 subscribers, Lite starts at $15 monthly or $12.50/month billed annually ($150/year). It allows up to ten times the subscriber maximum in monthly sends, one list, three landing pages, three automations, three users, and one custom segment. These caps may constrain a growing workflow.

At 500 subscribers, Plus starts at $30 monthly or $20/month billed annually ($240/year), with a twelve-times monthly send allowance and unlimited listed features. Unlimited is $899/month for high-volume requirements. Compare the added cost with limits your team would actually hit.

AWeber's integration directory includes CRM, ecommerce, landing-page, and productivity categories. Verify the particular integration, data fields, and automation trigger your operation requires.

Pros

  • Free offers a way to begin with up to 500 subscribers and one basic workflow.
  • Lite and Plus use stated subscriber-based send allowances.
  • The vendor documents forms, landing pages, automation, and an integration directory.

Tradeoffs

  • Free and Lite limit profiles, automations, pages, users, or segments; check each cap.
  • Subscriber-tier pricing can change as the active list grows; model a realistic next tier.
  • Plus starts above the entry paid prices shown for the competitors; broader limits matter if you use them.

MailerLite for small businesses

Current plans are Free, Comfort, Power, and Enterprise. Following the June 16, 2026 update, Free allows 250 active subscribers, 2,500 monthly emails, two seats, three automations, three forms, one landing page, and one website of up to five pages. Plan for growth near these limits.

Comfort starts at $12 per month and scales by subscriber tier. The vendor lists 50 automations, ten forms, and ten websites and landing pages combined. Comfort's sending allowance is ten times the ceiling of the selected subscriber tier. Power starts at $25 per month and lists unlimited monthly emails and feature use, subject to fair use. Enterprise is custom for larger subscriber counts.

The website and landing-page tools may reduce the number of separate products a small business evaluates. Limits remain plan-specific: verify published pages, active forms, and automations. Check the integration directory for your exact trigger and data sync.

Pros

  • Free combines campaigns, automations, a website, a landing page, and forms within stated limits.
  • Comfort and Power offer different growth paths: capped use and broader limits.
  • The vendor publishes plan boundaries and explains the effects of exceeding Free limits.

Tradeoffs

  • The 250-subscriber and 2,500-email Free ceilings may be reached by a growing list or frequent schedule.
  • Comfort limits active tools and sending by tier; Power's unlimited sending is subject to fair use.
  • Verify plan names and legacy-account details if migrating from an older account.

Brevo for small businesses

Brevo's plans are organized around email-send volume and associated stored-contact tiers. Free costs $0/month, stores up to 100,000 contacts, and allows 300 email sends per day. Marketing automation covers up to 2,000 contacts on Free, so the contact-storage ceiling does not mean everyone can receive a campaign on the same day.

Starter starts at $9/month and removes the Free daily send limit; price depends on monthly send tier. Standard starts at $18/month and lists unlimited-contact automation, A/B testing, advanced reports, and send-time optimization. Professional starts at $499/month. Verify tier and add-ons at checkout.

Brevo positions itself across email, SMS, WhatsApp, chat, and related customer communication; plan documentation lists transactional email and SMS. Confirm channel availability, country coverage, sending setup, and price for your workflow. No channel choice guarantees inbox placement or delivery outcomes.

Pros

  • The send-volume model can suit a business that forecasts campaigns by message count.
  • Free combines contact storage with a daily send cap and a separate automation-contact limit.
  • The vendor documents email alongside SMS and transactional messaging features.

Tradeoffs

  • The 300-per-day Free limit can constrain a campaign even when the account can store many contacts.
  • Compare monthly sends and stored contacts; subscriber totals alone are insufficient.
  • Check channel, add-on, and tier availability for your country and workflow.

Pricing and total operating cost

Information reviewed: October 5, 2026. The models are not directly comparable: AWeber prices by subscriber tier and send allowance, MailerLite by active subscribers and plan limits, and Brevo mainly by send volume. Use the table for starting prices; annual billing, taxes, add-ons, and the next tier can change the total.

Budget for active contacts, monthly sends, automations, seats, and any extra channels. Include tools you still need for CRM, booking, or ecommerce, then check billing terms and add-ons. No option is universally cheapest.

Before you commit: five checks

  1. Contact portability: Confirm how to export contacts, consent records, tags, custom fields, and suppression data.
  2. Consent and privacy: Map signup language, consent evidence, unsubscribe handling, retention, and your obligations in regions you serve.
  3. Domain authentication and sending controls: Check current setup guidance for your domain, authentication records, unsubscribe behavior, and account safeguards. This is an operational check, not a claim that one vendor delivers better.
  4. Integrations and workflow: Confirm the CRM, accounting, booking, ecommerce, or API events you need, including what happens when a contact changes or unsubscribes.
  5. Migration and support: Check import help, transferable history, support access, and any overlap needed during the move.
  6. Operations and handoff: Name the account owner, billing manager, and campaign publisher; verify roles and document who can step in when the usual operator is absent.

Run the same test before you buy

This is a reader evaluation procedure; Karasu did not run these tests across the three products.

  1. Import a small sample list with a duplicate; where safe, include an unsubscribed or suppressed contact and verify how it is handled.
  2. Build one real welcome workflow: signup trigger, delay, and an exit or stop condition.
  3. Send a representative campaign and check formatting, links, and sender/domain setup.
  4. Review whether the reports expose the metrics your team actually needs.
  5. Export your data and confirm contacts and relevant fields are portable.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best email marketing software for a small business?

There is no universal best: compare AWeber for subscriber-based email, MailerLite for a small-list campaign and site toolkit, or Brevo for send-volume and multichannel needs.

Is AWeber good for a small business?

It can fit a business seeking email, forms, landing pages, and autoresponders together; check the tier limits against your workflow.

Is MailerLite cheaper than AWeber?

Its Comfort starting price is lower than AWeber Lite's, but total cost depends on your subscriber tier, required features, billing term, and send allowance.

Is Brevo better for frequent email sending?

Evaluate it if you plan by monthly volume; estimate sends, stored contacts, automation, and add-ons before selecting a tier.

Which platform has the best free plan for a small business?

Match the limits to your needs: AWeber offers more subscribers but one workflow; MailerLite adds site tools at a smaller list cap; Brevo stores more contacts but caps daily sends and automation.

When should a small business pay for email automation?

Upgrade when a needed workflow exceeds Free limits or a paid feature saves enough staff time to justify its recurring cost.

Final recommendation by operating model

  • Traditional email marketing with subscriber-based planning: Evaluate AWeber if its forms, integrations, and autoresponder model match your workflow. Compare Lite caps with Plus.
  • Tiny list and simple low-cost campaign/site stack: Evaluate MailerLite while keeping the 250 active subscriber and 2,500 monthly email Free limits in view.
  • Sending-volume, transactional, or multichannel needs: Evaluate Brevo and price the expected send tier, automation boundary, and channels together.

Shortlist around the work your business needs to do, then verify plan limits and costs against that workflow.
