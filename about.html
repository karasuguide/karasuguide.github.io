ABOUT KARASU

Practical guidance for choosing and connecting software.

Karasu is an independent digital publication focused on software, automation, productivity, marketing technology, and modern business workflows.

Our goal is straightforward: help small businesses, creators, and lean teams understand what a tool does, where it fits, what it costs, and whether it is worth adding to their stack.

How we create content

We research official product information, documentation, pricing, public product updates, and relevant third-party sources. When we publish a comparison or recommendation, we aim to separate factual product information from our analysis.

We do not invent personal product experiences, customer results, or testimonials. Pricing and product features can change, so readers should confirm important details on the vendor’s official website before purchasing.

How Karasu may earn revenue

Some pages may contain affiliate links. If a reader purchases through one of those links, Karasu may receive a commission at no additional cost to the reader. Affiliate relationships do not determine the conclusions of our editorial content.
